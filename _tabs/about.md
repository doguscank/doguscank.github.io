I build computer vision and multimodal AI systems. My work connects model development with the engineering needed to put those models into useful applications.

At VidRush, I develop internal agentic AI tools, improve AI video production pipelines, and research models and methods for product development. Previously, I built production systems for document understanding and identity verification at GönderAL. Before that, I worked on perception and robotics software at DELIVERS.AI, including semantic segmentation deployed on NVIDIA Jetson Xavier across a fleet of autonomous delivery robots.

Alongside industry work, I research feature-space domain adaptation for semantic segmentation at ITU's Visual Perception Research Group while pursuing an MSc in Computer Engineering. My ongoing work focuses on synthetic-to-real transfer and visual foundation-model feature alignment.

My independent projects cover generative image editing, multimodal classification, language-model agents, scientific paper retrieval, and visual model integration. Earlier work in rover navigation, UAV simulation, and rocket avionics gave me experience with sensing, embedded systems, simulation, and technical leadership.

I'm interested in how learned visual features transfer across domains, how multimodal models can be combined into practical applications, and how to evaluate and deploy AI systems reliably.
